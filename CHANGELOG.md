## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-22

First public release.

- Menu bar timer with presets and custom durations from 1 second to 24 hours.
- Click-through progress line that shrinks from right to left.
- Half-point thickness, custom colors, opacity, placement, and display selection.
- Pause, resume, stop, restart, remaining percentage, and optional completion sound.
- Deadline-based recovery after sleep or relaunch.
- Adaptive display updates, an on-demand menu interface, and no periodic ticks while idle or paused.
- Universal macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel; macOS 14 or later.
- MIT-licensed source, automated tests, and build scripts.

The initial download is ad hoc signed and not notarized. Updates are manual.
